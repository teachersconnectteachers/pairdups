Child Safety Standards

PairdUps · Last updated: October 29, 2026

These are the published child safety standards for PairdUps ("PairdUps", "the app", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Frederick Rotgaus, an individual based in Ontario, Canada. PairdUps is an activity-partner platform where adults post short activity listings and connect to meet up in real life.

PairdUps has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content or conduct that sexualizes, endangers, grooms, or exploits a minor. This page explains the standards we uphold and how we act on them, in compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

1. PairdUps is for adults 18 and older

PairdUps is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and over. Minors are not permitted to create an account or use the service. Users must confirm they are 18 or older when registering, and the app is rated for mature audiences. Any account we identify as belonging to a minor will be removed.

2. Prohibited conduct and content

The following are strictly prohibited on PairdUps and will result in immediate removal and a permanent ban:

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or content that sexualizes, depicts, or endangers a minor.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to sexually exploit, lure, or arrange contact with a minor.
  • Using the platform to facilitate the trafficking, abuse, or endangerment of a child in any way.
  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to CSAE/CSAM content, or directing users off-platform for such purposes.
  • Impersonating a minor, or encouraging a minor to use the service.

3. How to report a child safety concern

Anyone can report a child safety concern, and we encourage immediate reporting. PairdUps provides in-app tools to report and block:

  • Report a user — from the messaging screen, use the menu to Report User.
  • Report a listing — use Report this listing on any activity post.
  • Block a user — use Block User to stop all contact from that person.

Report directly to us: You can also report any child safety concern at any time by emailing fredhockey23@gmail.com with the subject line "Child Safety Report". Reports are treated with urgency and confidentiality.

4. How we respond to reports

When we receive a report of CSAE, or otherwise become aware of such content or conduct, we act promptly to:

  • Review the report and remove the offending content as quickly as possible.
  • Permanently ban the account(s) responsible and block further access.
  • Preserve relevant information as needed to support investigations by the appropriate authorities.
  • Report confirmed CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant authorities, as described below.

5. Reporting to authorities

PairdUps complies with applicable child safety laws and cooperates with law enforcement. Where we identify apparent child sexual abuse material or exploitation, we report it to the appropriate national and regional authorities, which may include the Canadian Centre for Child Protection (Cybertip.ca), the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and/or local law enforcement, in accordance with the laws that apply to the affected users and to PairdUps.

6. Prevention

We work to prevent CSAE on the platform by restricting the service to adults, requiring age confirmation at sign-up, providing accessible in-app reporting and blocking tools, acting on reports, and removing accounts that violate these standards.
