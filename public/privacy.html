Privacy Policy

PairdUps · Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PairdUps ("PairdUps", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the PairdUps website and mobile application. PairdUps is operated by Frederick Rotgaus, an individual based in Ontario, Canada. By creating an account or using PairdUps, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

PairdUps is an activity-partner platform where users post short activity listings ("PairdUps") and connect with others to meet up for shared activities.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: your email address, name (or display name), age, and location (such as country, province/state, and city).
  • Profile information: your bio, interests, and photos you choose to upload (up to 12 photos for certain membership tiers).
  • Content you create: activity listings, messages you send to other users, and join requests.
  • Payment information: if you subscribe to a paid membership tier (Premium or Gold), payment is processed by our payment provider, Stripe. Stripe collects and processes your payment card details directly; PairdUps does not store your full card number or payment card details on our systems.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage information: activity within the app, such as listings you view or apply to, profile visits, and gamification data (experience points and badges).
  • Device and technical information: basic technical data needed to operate the service and deliver notifications, such as a device/notification identifier used by our push-notification provider.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To operate core features: showing listings in the Discover feed, enabling join requests, and delivering messages between users.
  • To process subscriptions and payments for paid membership tiers.
  • To send you service-related communications, such as a welcome email, notifications about new messages or join requests, and account or email-verification emails.
  • To provide optional features such as saved filters, custom theme colors, and alerts for new matching listings.
  • To maintain the safety and integrity of the platform, including preventing spam, fraud, and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

With other users

PairdUps is a social platform, so certain information is shared with other users by design. When you post a listing or appear in the Discover feed, other users can see your display name, age, location, photos, bio, and the content of your listings. Messages you send are visible to the recipient. Please only share information you are comfortable making visible to other users.

For legal reasons

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect the rights or safety of our users or the public, or investigate potential violations of our terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the service. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

How to delete your account and data: To delete your account and the personal data associated with it, contact us at the email address below with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", using the email address associated with your account. We will process your request and delete your personal data, except where we are required to retain certain information to comply with legal obligations.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data, and to withdraw consent. These rights are provided under applicable laws such as Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), where applicable. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

  • You can view and update most of your profile information directly within the app.
  • You can manage notification preferences within the app.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, including the use of encrypted connections and reputable service providers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

PairdUps is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. International Users

PairdUps is operated from Canada, and your information may be stored and processed in Canada, the United States, or other countries where our service providers operate. By using PairdUps, you consent to the transfer of your information to these locations.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of PairdUps after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
